# FAQ

## Is it free?
Yes. AI Video Upscaler+ is distributed free of charge without a subscription or per-video processing fee.

## Does it add a watermark?
No.

## Can it upscale to 4K?
Yes. 4K UHD output is supported.

## Can it upscale to 8K?
Yes. Supported output targets extend to 8K.

## Can it convert 30 FPS to 60 FPS?
Yes, using RIFE AI frame interpolation.

## Can it target 45 FPS?
Yes.

## Can upscaling and FPS enhancement be used together?
Yes.

## Does it support Intel GPUs?
Compatible Intel DirectML/D3D12 hardware is supported.

## Does it support AMD GPUs?
Compatible AMD DirectML/D3D12 hardware is supported.

## Does it support NVIDIA GPUs?
Compatible NVIDIA DirectML/D3D12 hardware is supported.

## Does it require CUDA?
No. The GPU inference path uses ONNX Runtime with DirectML.

## Are videos uploaded?
The AI video processing itself runs locally on the user's PC.

## Can it process old videos?
Yes. Older and low-resolution footage is a major use case.

## Can it process new videos?
Yes. Frame interpolation can be used with modern 24–30 FPS footage.

## Can AI recover missing original detail?
No. AI can reconstruct plausible detail, but cannot guarantee recovery of information that was never captured.

## Can frame interpolation create artifacts?
Yes, especially with complex motion, occlusions, reflections, transparency and scene cuts.

## Does it support batch processing?
Yes.
